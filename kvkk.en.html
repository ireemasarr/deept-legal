Your Data Rights

Under the GDPR and Türkiye’s KVKK · 5 October 2026

This notice summarizes your rights over your personal data under the EU GDPR and Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK). For full details, please see our Privacy Policy.

Data controller: DEEPT · deept.app1@gmail.com

Where the service is offered

DEEPT is not offered in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, and is not directed at users there. The GDPR statements below remain accurate for anyone who nonetheless accesses the App.

Age requirement

DEEPT is for users aged 13 and over; children under 13 must not use the App.

Data we process

  • Identity and contact: email address, username
  • Images: a profile photo if you upload one, plus images you upload to your vision board or the community
  • Usage: study sessions, progress, virtual coins and collection, goals and vision board, community content
  • Exam information: the exams you follow and, if you choose to enter it, your reported score and rank
  • Reports and moderation records: reports you file, blocks you set, and any enforcement recorded about your account
  • Technical: push token, basic session data, and basic usage records such as which screens you open
  • Crash and diagnostic data: technical error reports generated when the App closes unexpectedly
  • Advertising identifier: the device advertising identifier used to serve ads (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android). Ads are non-personalized.

Purposes and legal basis

We process your data to provide the service (performance of a contract), to review reports and enforce our community rules, to detect and fix crashes, to keep the service secure and improve it (our legitimate interest), and — where required, such as for notifications — with your consent.

Sharing

We share data with Supabase (hosting and infrastructure), Google AdMob (ad serving), Google Cloud Vision (automatic screening of uploaded profile and community images), Expo (push notification delivery: push token, sender name and message preview), Sentry (crash and error monitoring) and Apple and Google (sign-in, notifications and store services), only to provide the service and with appropriate safeguards. Sentry receives technical diagnostic data only — no name, email or IP address — and stores it in the European Union. Google and Expo process data mainly in the United States; such transfers are made under the conditions and safeguards of Article 9 of the KVKK. We do not sell your data.

What other users can see

Your username, profile photo, community posts, room messages and your position in the weekly study ranking are visible to other users. If you choose to enter a score for an exam, that score and rank are visible to other users on the exam’s results table together with your username and profile photo; entering a score is optional and you can delete it from inside the App. Your goals, vision board, direct messages and study notes are private to you.

Retention and backups

Your account and study data are kept while your account is active. Some records are deleted automatically: screen and event records after 30 days; system metrics and ad records after 90 days; room messages after 180 days; direct messages after 365 days. Our database is backed up daily and backups are retained for 90 days, solely for disaster recovery.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict the processing of, object to the processing of, and port your personal data.

How to exercise them: delete your data in the App via Profile → Privacy → Delete account, or email deept.app1@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. See also the Privacy Policy and the support page.
