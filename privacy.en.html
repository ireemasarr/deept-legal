Privacy Policy

DEEPT mobile app · Effective: 5 July 2026

We care about your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect when you use DEEPT (the “App”), why and how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Data controller: DEEPT (İrem Asar)
Contact: deept.app1@gmail.com

Information we collect

We only collect what the App needs to work:

  • Account: your email address and chosen username. If you use “Sign in with Apple”, the identifier Apple provides.
  • Profile: a profile photo if you choose to upload one, and your appearance preferences.
  • Study data: your focus sessions (subject, duration and date), streaks, virtual coins and the sea creatures you unlock.
  • Community content: posts, comments, likes, room and direct messages, and files or images you upload.
  • Push token: your device push token, so we can send reminder notifications.
  • Basic technical data: the minimal device information needed for your session and the App to run properly.

The App does not take real-money payments; the coins inside it are entirely virtual and have no monetary value.

Why we use it

We process your data to create your account and sign you in; to save your study stats, collection and progress; to run community, room and messaging features; to send reminder notifications if you ask us to; to improve the App and keep it secure; and to show ads.

Under the GDPR, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the service), our legitimate interest (security and improvement), and, where needed (e.g. notifications), your consent.

Advertising

The App shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads are non-personalized by default, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. You can review AdMob’s privacy practices here.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only when needed to provide the service, with these trusted providers:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication and file hosting infrastructure.
  • Google AdMob — ad serving.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in, notifications and store services.

Beyond this, we share information only where legally required.

Data retention

Your data is stored securely on Supabase’s cloud infrastructure and kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is permanently erased.

Your rights

Under GDPR and KVKK you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its processing, and receive a copy of it.

Deleting your account and data is easy: in the App, go to Profile → Privacy → Delete account and your account and all related data are permanently removed. You can also email deept.app1@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

DEEPT is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has shared information with us, we will delete it promptly.

Security

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including row-level access control, encrypted connections and access restrictions. No system is entirely risk-free, but we take protecting your information seriously.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes in the App, and the current version is always on this page.

Contact

You can reach us any time at deept.app1@gmail.com.
