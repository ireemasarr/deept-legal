Privacy Policy

DEEPT mobile app · Effective: 5 October 2026

We care about your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect when you use DEEPT (the “App”), why and how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Data controller: DEEPT
Contact: deept.app1@gmail.com

Where the service is offered

DEEPT is not offered in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom; the App is not published on the stores in those countries and is not directed at users there. For anyone who nonetheless accesses the App, the GDPR statements in this policy remain accurate and continue to apply.

Age requirement

DEEPT is for users aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, you must not use the App or send us any information.

Information we collect

We only collect what the App needs to work:

  • Account: your email address and chosen username. If you use “Sign in with Apple”, the identifier Apple provides.
  • Profile: a profile photo if you choose to upload one, and your appearance preferences.
  • Study data: your focus sessions (subject, duration and date), streaks, virtual coins and the sea creatures you unlock.
  • Community content: posts, comments, likes, room and direct messages, and files or images you upload.
  • Goals and vision board: the goals you write, your progress counts, the cards you add to your board and any images you upload to them.
  • Exam information: the exams you choose to follow and — if you choose to enter it — the score and rank you report.
  • Reports and moderation records: records created when you report content or a user, or block someone; and, if an enforcement action was taken about your account (warning, mute, restriction), the record of it.
  • Push token: your device push token, so we can send reminder and new-message notifications. A message notification contains the sender’s name and a short preview of the message.
  • Basic technical data: the minimal device information needed for your session and the App to run properly, plus basic usage records such as which screens you open and errors you encounter.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: error reports generated when the App closes unexpectedly (the type of error, where it happened, the app version and device model). These reports are sent to Sentry, a third-party error monitoring service. Sentry is configured not to receive identifying information such as your name, email or IP address, and the data is stored in the European Union.
  • Advertising identifier: Google AdMob uses your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android). See the “Advertising” section below.

The App does not take real-money payments; the coins inside it are entirely virtual and have no monetary value.

Why we use it

We process your data to create your account and sign you in; to save your study stats, collection, goals and progress; to run community, room and messaging features; to send reminder notifications if you ask us to; to review reports and enforce our community rules; to detect and fix crashes; to improve the App and keep it secure; and to show ads.

Under the GDPR, our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the service), our legitimate interest (security and improvement), and, where needed (e.g. notifications), your consent.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Watching rewarded ads is entirely optional.

To serve ads, AdMob uses your device advertising identifier: the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) on iOS and the AAID (Android Advertising ID) on Android. Data about ad delivery is sent to Google, which processes it under its own privacy policy.

Ads are non-personalized. That means the ads you see are not selected based on your interests or past behaviour; we do not track you across other apps or websites and do not build a profile for that purpose. You can reset or limit your device advertising identifier at any time in your phone’s settings. You can review AdMob’s privacy practices here.

What other users can see

DEEPT has a community side, so some information is visible not only to you but to other users of the App:

  • Your username, profile photo and the study statistics shown on your profile.
  • Your community posts, comments, room messages and your position in the weekly study ranking.
  • Your exam scores: if you choose to enter a score for an exam, that score and rank are visible to other users on that exam’s results table, together with your username and profile photo. Entering a score is never required, and you can delete a score you entered from inside the App — it disappears from the table as well.

Your goals, vision board, direct messages and study notes are private to you; other users cannot see them.

Reporting, blocking and moderation

To keep the community safe, users can report each other’s content and block each other. When you report a post or a message, the reported content, the reporting account and the time are stored as a report record and reviewed by us. When you block someone, this is also recorded for our review. Profile photos and community images are automatically screened before they are published; an image found to be objectionable is not published.

Following that review we may remove content that breaks the rules and apply enforcement to the account involved, such as a warning, a temporary mute, or a ban. These enforcement records are retained so we can assess repeated behaviour and prevent abuse. When you block someone, that choice is also stored on your account; you can remove a block under Profile → Privacy → Blocked people.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only when needed to provide the service, with these trusted providers:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication and file hosting infrastructure.
  • Google AdMob — ad serving (advertising identifier and ad delivery data).
  • Google Cloud Vision — automatic screening of the profile photos and community images you upload for objectionable content (such as nudity or violence). Images are sent only for this check.
  • Expo — delivering push notifications to your device (push token, sender name and message preview).
  • Sentry — crash and error monitoring. It receives technical diagnostic data only, is configured not to receive identifying information, and stores its data in the European Union.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in, notifications and store services.

Some of these providers (for example Google and Expo) process data outside Türkiye, mainly in the United States; such transfers are made with the safeguards required by applicable law.

Beyond this, we share information only where legally required.

Data retention

Your data is stored securely on Supabase’s cloud infrastructure. Your account and study data are kept while your account is active. In addition, some records are deleted automatically after the following periods:

  • Screen and event records (which screens you open, errors encountered): 30 days
  • System metrics and ad records (rewarded ads watched and rewards granted): 90 days
  • Room messages: 180 days
  • Direct messages: 365 days

When you delete your account, your data is permanently erased. See the account deletion page for what is deleted and for the limited records that may be kept in anonymised form to meet legal obligations.

Backups

To protect against data loss, our database is backed up daily and backups are retained for 90 days. When you delete your account, your data is removed from the live system immediately; copies in backups taken before that date disappear when the backup period expires, within 90 days at the latest. Backups are kept solely for disaster recovery and are used for no other purpose.

Your rights

Under GDPR and KVKK you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its processing, and receive a copy of it.

Deleting your account and data is easy: in the App, go to Profile → Privacy → Delete account and your account and all related data are permanently removed. You can also email deept.app1@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

DEEPT is for users aged 13 and over; children under 13 must not use the App. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has shared information with us, we will delete the account and the data promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us information, please write to deept.app1@gmail.com.

Security

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including row-level access control, encrypted connections and access restrictions. No system is entirely risk-free, but we take protecting your information seriously.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes in the App, and the current version is always on this page.

Contact

You can reach us any time at deept.app1@gmail.com. You can also check our support page for frequently asked questions and help.
